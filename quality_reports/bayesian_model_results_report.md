# 📊 Bayesian Model Results Report

## 1. Executive Summary
The Bayesian analysis examines the interaction between MEP characteristics and the responsiveness of policy positions to external indicators (CMP and MIPEX). 

**Key Finding:** There is a strong and statistically significant positive effect of being a **Right-Wing Nationalist** and **Liberal** on the responsiveness of policy positions. Notably, the "minority" status shows a high positive estimate in the CMP interaction, suggesting that MEPs from minority groups may exhibit higher sensitivity to these indicators.

---

## 2. Detailed Model Analysis

### 📈 CMP Interaction Model
The CMP Interaction model evaluates how MEP attributes influence the relationship between the CMP center and policy positions.

| Variable | Estimate | 95% Credible Interval | Significance |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Right-Wing Nationalists** | `0.600` | `[0.222, 0.972]` | ✅ Significant |
| **Liberals** | `0.492` | `[0.146, 0.830]` | ✅ Significant |
| **Minority Status** | `1.059` | `[0.080, 1.917]` | ✅ Significant |
| **Eurosceptic Cons.** | `0.224` | `[-0.177, 0.620]` | ❌ Not Sig. |
| **Misery Index** | `-0.005` | `[-0.023, 0.013]` | ❌ Not Sig. |

**Observations:**
- The highest positive effect is seen among **minority MEPs** ($\beta \approx 1.06$), indicating a potentially stronger alignment or responsiveness.
- **Right-Wing Nationalists** ($\beta \approx 0.60$) and **Liberals** ($\beta \approx 0.49$) are also significantly positive, suggesting these groups are more responsive to the CMP metrics than the baseline.

### 📈 MIPEX Interaction Model
The MIPEX Interaction model looks at the responsiveness to the MIPEX (migration policy) indicators.

| Variable | Estimate | 95% Credible Interval | Significance |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Right-Wing Nationalists** | `0.525` | `[0.158, 0.899]` | ✅ Significant |
| **Liberals** | `0.429` | `[0.080, 0.751]` | ✅ Significant |
| **Minority Status** | `0.327` | `[-0.596, 1.152]` | ❌ Not Sig. |
| **Minority $\times$ MIPEX Center**| `0.066` | `[0.016, 0.118]` | ✅ Significant |

**Observations:**
- Similar to the CMP model, **Right-Wing Nationalists** and **Liberals** show significant positive responsiveness.
- While the main effect of "minority" is not significant here, the **interaction between minority status and the MIPEX center** is positive and significant ($\beta \approx 0.066, p < 0.05$), suggesting a nuanced responsiveness for minority MEPs specifically tied to the MIPEX center.

---

## 3. Comparative Conclusions
1. **Consistency:** The responsiveness of **Right-Wing Nationalists** and **Liberals** is consistent across both CMP and MIPEX frameworks.
2. **Minority Dynamics:** Minority MEPs show a very strong general responsiveness in the CMP model, but a more specific, interaction-driven responsiveness in the MIPEX model.
3. **Non-Factors:** Gender and the Misery Index do not appear to play a significant role in the responsiveness of these models.
