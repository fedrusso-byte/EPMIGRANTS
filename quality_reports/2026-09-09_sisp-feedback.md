## Purpose

At the 2026 SISP (Società Italiana di Scienza Politica) annual conference, discussants judged the
theoretical rationale for **H3** — the conditional hypothesis that the link between MEPs' migratory or
racialised minority background and discrimination-related parliamentary attention strengthens as the
national anti-discrimination framework becomes more comprehensive — to be **too thin**. This memo
records that feedback in writing so that it can anchor the planned theory revision.

**Why it matters:** H3 is currently the only hypothesis supported by the data (interaction β = 0.0658,
95% CrI [0.016, 0.117]), so its theoretical foundation is the paper's weakest load-bearing wall.
